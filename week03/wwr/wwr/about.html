Ngalonde Rafting Adventures

Ngalonde Rafting Adventures provides exciting and memorable white water rafting experiences for individuals, families, and groups. Our mission is to provide safe outdoor adventures while helping people enjoy the beauty of nature.

History

Ngalonde Rafting Adventures was created from a passion for rivers, nature, and outdoor adventure. Our company brings people together through exciting rafting experiences while focusing on safety, teamwork, and excellent customer service.
